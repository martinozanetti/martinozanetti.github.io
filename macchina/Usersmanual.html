Basics for Guest Users

⚠️ IN CASE OF EMERGENCY:
FIRST AID KIT and a safety reflective vest in the left door.
A list of numbers you might need is in the copilot's dashboard, in the black envelope, on a credit-card sized sheet.

  • IMPORTANT DOCUMENTS: car registration document and insurance are in the copilot's dashboard, inside the black envelope.
  • BEFORE STARTING: check that the car looks ok, to be sure that lights are working/tires are inflated (and that any possible existing damage can't be attributed to you later). Check the mirrors and adjust the seat.
  • LIGHTS: turn the knob on the left of the steering wheel.
  • HIGH BEAMS: push the lever on the left of the steering wheel to switch on/off (half way for just a flash).
  • WINDSHIELD WIPERS: press on top of the lever on the right of the steering wheel to spray soaped water and activate wipers. Use up/down or push/pull to control back or front wipers.
  • RADIO and MUSIC: aux cable → press aux button. Bluetooth → phone>add> (consult the manual for details).
    If you need to delete a registered device, please avoid ours (FP3, FP4, …).
  • NAVIGATION: there is a nice built-in phone holder above the radio controls that can be lifted to secure your phone.
  • CHARGING your phone: USB-C and iPhone cables are in the small compartment above the radio panel.
  • DRIVING: not much to say, just follow the rules and ride safe 🙂

Cruise control: press the “+” button to switch it on, press it again to activate it. It gets deactivated when you brake.

  • FUEL: Use gasoline (benzin E95), which is the green pump. The car will be returned to you with a full tank, so please try to return it full. This one in Praterstern is near and open 24h and relatively cheap.
  • PARKING: in the 2nd district, except Praterstrasse and Taborstrasse. The closer to Rueppgasse 27, the better (usually easy to find a spot there). Let us know where it’s parked. During the weekend, the car can be parked in the other districts as well (but check the signs!)

Insurance

  • Basic mobility liability insurance for personal injury, property damage, and consequent financial losses → up to 20 millions €
  • Fire/Explosion
  • Theft/Robbery
  • Natural Hazards
  • Wild and Domestic Animals
  • Glass Breakage (but the first €350.00 are on us)
  • Other perils (additional coverage based on the underlying contract terms)
